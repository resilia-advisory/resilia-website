Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Debt Repayment Scheme?

The Debt Repayment Scheme is a pre-bankruptcy scheme administered under Singapore's insolvency framework for eligible debtors. Eligibility and acceptance depend on the applicable requirements and assessment by the relevant authorities.

Am I automatically eligible for DRS?

No. Eligibility depends on your individual circumstances and the applicable statutory requirements. Being in debt does not automatically mean that a person will be placed on the DRS.

Can Resilia guarantee that my DRS application will be approved?

No. Resilia Advisory provides advisory and support services but cannot guarantee acceptance into any debt or insolvency arrangement.

Can I speak to someone before deciding?

Yes. Contact us via WhatsApp to discuss your circumstances and determine the appropriate next step.
